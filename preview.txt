› why is the sky blue?

Sunlight looks white, but it is a mix of every colour. As it enters the
atmosphere, it scatters off air molecules, and blue scatters far more than
red because its wavelength is shorter.

That scattered blue reaches your eyes from every direction, so the whole sky
glows. At sunset the light travels much further through the air, the blue is
scattered away, and what is left is orange and red.
